What is a Generative AI Product Engineer?

Generative AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that generate new content — text, code, images, audio — using large language models trained on vast datasets. A Generative AI Product Engineer goes beyond using these systems: they design and build production-grade AI applications using LLMs, RAG pipelines, AI Agents, vector databases, and cloud infrastructure. They engineer products that solve specific business problems at scale — AI chatbots, document intelligence tools, AI SaaS platforms, automation agents, and intelligent workflows. Engineers who can build these systems — not just prompt them — are the rarest and most in-demand AI professionals in India's current market.
